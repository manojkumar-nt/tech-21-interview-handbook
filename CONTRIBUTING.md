# Contributing to Tech-21 Interview Handbook

Thank you for your interest in the Tech-21 Series!

## Feedback and Corrections
If you find a typo, outdated syntax, or a clarification in any of the free sampler chapters:
1. Open a GitHub Issue detailing the book, chapter, and suggestion.
2. For small typos, feel free to open a Pull Request.

## Community Guidelines
- Please keep discussions focused on high-signal technical depth.
- Be respectful and constructive in all issue discussions.

Star the repo to show your support! ⭐
